## 13. MNC INTERVIEW DEFENSIBILITY (H5-L)

A candidate presenting SkyVanta AI can confidently defend every architectural decision:
- **Why YOLO + Motion Fusion?** Deep learning provides high-semantic classification under appearance changes, while background subtraction and optical flow provide frame-rate responsiveness and zero-shot motion detection even during detector dropouts.
- **Why Lie Group $\text{SO}(3)$ / $\text{SE}(3)$ over Euler Angles?** Avoids gimbal lock, eliminates coordinate singularities, enables geodesic interpolation, and supports mathematically exact error-state perturbation injection.
- **Why Error-State EKF (ESEKF) instead of Standard EKF?** Nominal state propagates non-linear kinematics with zero singularity; error state evolves linearly near the origin, avoiding large linearization errors and preserving covariance consistency.
- **Why Chi-Squared ($\chi^2$) Innovation Gating?** Prevents filter divergence caused by visual occlusion jumps, false ArUco marker detections, or multi-path noise by statistically rejecting measurement outliers before covariance assimilation.
- **Why Decouple Safety Supervisor from Estimation?** Prevents corrupted state estimates from bypassing mission safety rules; safety invariants independently verify raw sensor health, estimation uncertainty bounds, and spatial velocities before authorizing descent.
- **Why Command Rate Limiting & Monotonic Sequences?** Protects autopilot command queues from buffer overflow, packet storms, and replay attacks.
- **Why Simulation First?** Enables thousands of deterministic Monte Carlo failure scenarios (wind gusts, sensor dropouts, latency spikes) to be verified without risk to physical hardware.
